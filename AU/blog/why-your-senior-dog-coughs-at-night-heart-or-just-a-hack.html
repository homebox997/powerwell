Why Your Senior Dog Coughs at Night: Heart or Just a Hack?

July 31, 2026 · 6 min read · PawWell Editorial

Its 2 a.m. and that hacking starts again

Youve just settled your big old Labrador onto his favourite mat beside your bed. You hear that soft, wet cough. He coughs, gags a little, shifts around, then coughs again. Youre both wide awake now, and youre wonderingis this just a leftover splash from his water bowl, or something more serious?

For a large senior dog, a cough that appears at night (or gets worse when hes lying down) often points towards the heart. Not every cough is a panic button, but a persistent night cough in a dog over seven deserves a vets ears and eyes. This guide will walk you through the difference between a harmless hack and a heart-related warning, what your vet will check, and simple things you can do at home to keep your big fella comfortable.

Why lying down can turn a quiet heart into a noisy one

When your dog is upright, gravity helps keep fluid in the lower half of his body. Once he lies flat to sleep, that fluid can shift up into his chest. If his heart isnt pumping as strongly as it used tosomething common in senior large breedsfluid can pool in the lungs. Thats called pulmonary oedema, and it makes the airways tickly and wet. The result: a soft, moist cough, often with a bit of gagging or wheezing.

In many older dogs, this starts with a leaky heart valve (myxomatous mitral valve disease, or MMVD). Think of it like a door in the heart that doesnt shut fully, so a little blood squirts backwards with every beat. The heart works harder, thickens, and eventually struggles to move blood efficiently. In large breeds like Boxers, Dobermanns or Irish Wolfhounds, another condition is more common: dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM), where the heart muscle becomes weak and baggy. Both can lead to fluid buildup and a night-time cough.

Harmless cough or heart sign? How to tell

Not every cough is a heart red flag. A tickle from dust, a bit of post-water-bowl sputter, or even a case of kennel cough can all cause night coughing. The clues that make a vet prick up their ears:

  • Timing: A heart cough often worsens at night or after the dog settles down. A dry, hacking cough that comes on after a walk and fades might be more airway-related.
  • Sound: Heart coughs are often soft, wet, or gurgly. Kennel cough tends to be loud, dry, and paroxysmal (often ending with a honk or gag).
  • Other signs: Is your big dog panting more at rest? Does he tire out on walks he used to smash? Has his belly started looking a bit pot-bellied? Any of these alongside a night cough is a strong nudge to book a vet visit.

What your vet looks for

Your vet will first listen to your dogs heart and lungs with a stethoscope. A heart murmurthat whooshing sound of a leaky valveis often the earliest clue. Theyll check gum colour (pale or grey gums can mean poor oxygen circulation) and ask about his energy levels and appetite. If they suspect fluid, they might suggest a chest X-ray to see the heart size and any cloudiness in the lungs. All of this helps determine whether the cough is from the heart, the airways, or something else entirely.

One familys early catch: an illustrative scenario

Illustrative scenario not a real patient. Max, a 9-year-old Golden Retriever from Brisbane, started giving a soft, wet cough every night about an hour after lights out. His owner Sarah brushed it off as a dusty rug. When Max began panting while just resting on his bed during the day, Sarah booked a vet check. The vet found a grade 3 heart murmur and, on X-ray, a slightly enlarged heart with early fluid in the lungs. With medication and careful home monitoringespecially counting his sleeping breathsMaxs cough eased within a week. Hes back to his chilled, early-morning beach walks, just a bit slower.

Home comfort while you wait for the vet

You can make your dog more comfortable tonight, but remember: these tips dont replace a vet assessment if the cough is persistent.

  • Elevate their head: A firm, raised pillow or a rolled-up towel under the bed can help fluid stay away from the chest while they sleep.
  • Keep the air cool and gentle: Use a fan or open a window to circulate fresh air. Thick, humid air makes breathing harder.
  • Watch the treats: High-salt snacks (deli meats, cheese, some commercial dog treats) force the body to hold onto fluid, which can overload a struggling heart. Stick to their normal, vet-approved diet.
  • Short, quiet walks only: Let your dog set the pace. A slow saunter on flat ground is fine, but skip the off-leash park or long hills while youre waiting for the vet appointment.

Your home monitoring superpower: count those sleeping breaths

The single most useful thing you can do at homeand vets absolutely love thisis to track your dogs sleeping respiratory rate. When a dog is in deep sleep (not dreaming, not just dozing), count how many times their chest rises in 15 seconds, then multiply that by four. Do it at the same time each day when theyre properly snoozing. A normal sleeping rate is usually under 30 breaths per minute. If you consistently see numbers above 3035, or the count is creeping up from their usual baseline, its a sign that fluid may be building up. Phone your vet even before the cough worsensthis simple habit often catches heart changes days ahead of obvious symptoms.

When to call your vet right away

Some signs mean you shouldnt wait for a booked appointment. If your dog shows any of the following, ring your vet immediately or head to an emergency clinic:

  • Open-mouth breathing or gasping at rest
  • Blue, grey, or pale gums
  • Collapse or sudden weakness
  • Inability to settle or lying in unusual positions to breathe
  • A resting respiratory rate consistently over 40 breaths per minute
  • Swollen belly that has appeared over a few days

A gentle push towards the vet

If your big senior dogs night cough has hung around for more than a few nights, or youve spotted any of the other signs mentioned, please book a vet visit. Early heart disease in dogs can be managed well for months to years with the right support, but leaving it unchecked lets fluid build up. The earlier you start, the more comfortable your mate stays. Frequently Asked Questions

Is a night cough always caused by heart failure?
No. Other problems like chronic bronchitis, collapsing trachea, or kennel cough can cause coughing that worsens at night. However, in a large senior dog, a persistent wet night cough with other signs like exercise intolerance needs a heart investigation.

Whats the difference between a heart cough and kennel cough?
Kennel cough is usually a loud, dry, honking cough that can end with a gag. A heart cough tends to be softer, moister, and appears especially after lying down. Your vet can distinguish them by listening and may suggest imaging if needed.

How do I count my dogs sleeping breaths properly?
Wait until they are truly asleeptwitching, eye movement, and irregular breathing mean theyre likely dreaming. Watch the chest rise and fall; one rise plus one fall is one breath. Count for 15 seconds and multiply by 4. Write down the number daily to spot trends.
